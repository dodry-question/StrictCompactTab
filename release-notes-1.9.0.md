Version 1.9.0 introduces a major visual and ergonomic redesign of the Settings modal, transforming it into an elegant, left-sliding glassmorphic sidebar panel. This sliding sidebar incorporates new hardware-accelerated animations on the GPU, guaranteeing locked 60 FPS transitions without visual stutter even on low-end laptops. The right column shortcut editor has been thoroughly optimized with safe paddings and box-sizing properties, completely eliminating horizontal scrolls and removing all vertical partition lines. Stealth Mode has been polished for enhanced legibility, raising the idle opacity of shortcut icons to 85% and the clock, weather, and search bar widgets to 70% with smooth hover transitions. Finally, typography contrast and label readability have been upgraded across Dark, Light, and Adaptive themes to ensure a highly clear and professional dashboard experience.
